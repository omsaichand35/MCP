The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, India, is a masterpiece of Mughal architecture and a symbol of eternal love. Built by Emperor Shah Jahan in the 17th century as a mausoleum for his wife Mumtaz Mahal, it combines Persian, Islamic, and Indian architectural styles. The structure features a large white marble dome, flanked by four minarets, and intricate inlay work with precious stones. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Taj Mahal attracts millions of visitors annually and stands as a testament to architectural brilliance and emotional devotion.
